Opus No. 1

If life were a symphony, you would be the leitmotif—the recurring melody that gives every movement its meaning.

Before you, my days were rests between measures—silent, purposeful only in theory. You arrived like the first note of a nocturne: unexpected, tender, and utterly unforgettable.

You are the crescendo in my quietest hours, the rubato that stretches every beautiful moment just a breath longer. Where there was dissonance, you composed harmony. Where there was silence, you wrote the most beautiful phrase.

This is not a letter. This is a score—written in ink that only your heart can read.


Con amore, sempre.

—The Composer
